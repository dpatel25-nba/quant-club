01 / About us

About the club

The Goizueta Portfolio Management Club gives students practical experience building, analyzing and managing investment portfolios.

Members study asset allocation, diversification and performance across asset classes, with an emphasis on managing a complete portfolio.

01

Portfolio projects

Build portfolios for different investor goals and risk profiles. Evaluate allocations using measures such as return, volatility, correlation and Sharpe ratios.

02

Market discussions

Discuss market developments and their implications for investment decisions. Apply portfolio concepts through research and case studies.

03

Research and presentations

Prepare portfolio reports and present investment strategies to fellow members. Opportunities may also include portfolio competitions.

02 / Our organization

Our teams

Members join one of four teams. Applicants may express interest in more than one.

01

Systematic Portfolio Management

Develop and test quantitative models and rules-based investment strategies. This team also builds the research tools on this website.

02

Fundamental Portfolio Management

Research companies, sectors and asset classes to compare investment opportunities and inform portfolio allocations.

03

Asset Allocation and Risk

Study how capital is allocated across investments and how diversification, volatility, correlation and concentration affect portfolio risk.

04

Investor Relations

Coordinate outreach to alumni, faculty, industry professionals and sponsors, along with speakers and external opportunities for members.

        Style Rotation

        Research snapshotKen French portfolios · FRED macro inputs

        Composite factor outlook

        Horizon-specific models rank cumulative future style-spread P&L. CSM fits pooled cross-sectional relationships; TRM fits each factor's recent technical history. ECM uses macro conditions. DMA retains its original four-model technical blend.

        Composite score: 25% CSM + 25% DMA + 25% TRM + 25% ECM. Each model's ranks become a 0–100 score, then are averaged. Higher is stronger relative consensus; 100 means unanimous first place. This is not an expected return or confidence probability. The models share information, so their votes are not independent.

        Working definitions: TRM = tactical regression modeling; ECM = economic cycle modeling, using market-based macro proxies. ECM is a provisional project definition, not an error-correction model.

        Methodology & Findings
        FindingsTiming across eras Portfolio interpretationWeighting & macro Reports & data

        What the research establishes

        Independent public-data tests of the summer style-rotation hypotheses. All experiments are retrospective.

        Baseline corrected

        One formation date, one next-month target

        Raw and hedged features have separate files. Calendar alignment, source identity, delayed feedback and future-data mutation are checked before scoring.

        Weighting audited

        Equal weighting is a demanding reference

        Full covariance weighting underperforms equal weighting in the primary audit. Removing exact opposite predictors repairs covariance rank but does not repair GK scores.

        Timing qualified

        Long-history timing is stronger than recent timing

        Era and publication-delay checks weaken the case for a stable recent edge. Wide intervals prevent a firm claim that timing declined after 2000.

        No established improvement

        Selection, macro and portfolio hurdles remain

        Nested selection and the fixed macro blend do not establish improvements over their references. The portfolio comparison does not establish an advantage over a frozen allocation.

        Timing across eras

        Headline Rank IC combines persistent style preferences with date-matched timing. Static IC is the all-month-pairing expectation; dynamic IC subtracts it. IC × 100 is a ranking score, not a return.

        Trailing ten-year IC

        Window end dates fall in the selected period; each point uses the preceding 120 target months, including earlier dates where needed. Windows overlap and are descriptive. Static IC is recomputed within each window.

        Portfolio interpretation

        Centered technical ranks become gross-one style-spread allocations. The frozen reference uses 60 pre-evaluation allocation months, then holds fixed. Equal style holds +1/15 of every signed spread.

        Cumulative P&L on fixed reference capital

        Profits are not reinvested. These are additive research P&L streams, not compounded account returns. Costs apply to changes in style allocations; stock-level turnover, financing, borrowing and internal hedge maintenance are unmodeled. Period slices inherit the original trading path.

        Weighting, selection and macro evidence

        These studies have their own fixed samples. The panel control applies throughout; publication delay applies to selection and macro. Evaluation period and cost controls apply to timing and P&L.

        Inspect all 18 technical predictors
        Marginal confidence intervals are not simultaneous. Primary p-values use the adjustment family specified in each report. Publication delays are scenarios on existing index data, not historical holdings or release vintages. Full specifications and limitations are below.

        Reports, specifications and data

        The complete research narrative, experiment definitions, Python source and exported result tables. Downloads use the same authenticated snapshot as the dashboard.
